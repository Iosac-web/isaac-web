About Primeval Pets

Founded in 2004, Primeval Pets has grown from a small passion project into Southern Oregon's most trusted exotic pet specialist. Our founder started with a single reptile room and a mission to provide healthier, ethically-bred animals to our community.

Today, we stock over 200 species of reptiles, amphibians, birds, and small mammals—all captive-bred and health-checked. Our knowledgeable staff includes certified herpetologists, exotics veterinarians, and decades of combined hands-on experience.
